About the game

Hexonia is a turn-based 4X strategy game on a hex map: explore through the fog of war, grow cities, lead squads into battle and defeat AI rivals by domination, prosperity or by taking their capitals. The game works fully offline.

Frequently asked questions

Where is my progress saved?
On your device. To move it to another device, use Menu → Transfer progress: it gives you a code that you enter on the other device.

How do I change the language?
The game uses your device language (English, Russian, Ukrainian, German, Spanish or French; otherwise English). You can switch it on the main menu.

The interface is too small or too large.
Change Interface size on the main menu or in the pause menu.

I found a bug.
Please email us with your device model, iOS/Android version and what happened — a screenshot helps a lot.
