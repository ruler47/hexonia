Privacy Policy

Hexonia: Hex Strategy · last updated October 15, 2026
SupportPrivacy Policy
Short version: Hexonia does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal data.

Data we collect

None. The game has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not use third-party SDKs that collect data.

Data stored on your device

Your saved games, campaign progress, game history and settings (language, sound, interface size) are stored only locally on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Progress transfer codes

The optional Transfer progress feature creates a text code containing your saved games. The code is generated on your device and is only shared if you choose to copy it yourself.

Purchases

If in-app purchases are offered in the future, payments are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We do not receive or store your payment information.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated date.
